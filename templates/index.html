Why Accuracy Alone is Misleading in NIDS Security Theory

1. The Accuracy Paradox: In operational enterprise networks, 95% to 99% of flows are benign. A trivial model predicting "Normal" 100% of the time achieves 99% accuracy yet permits 100% of cyber attacks to breach the organization.

2. False Alarms vs. Missed Breaches: Low precision causes SOC Alert Fatigue where analysts dismiss alarms. Low recall means stealthy cyber intrusions slip past the perimeter unnoticed.

3. Sub-Category Skew in UNSW-NB15: Massive traffic volumes belong to 'Generic' or 'Exploits', while devastating attacks like 'Worms', 'Shellcode', and 'Backdoors' constitute less than 1% of flows.

4. Consensus Defense: When both Random Forest and Autoencoder trigger an alert, confidence is maximized (Critical Intrusion).
